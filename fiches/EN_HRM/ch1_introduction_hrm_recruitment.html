Revision Sheet — HRM Ch 1

Introduction to HRM & Recruitment

1. HRM Definitions

Peretti (1977): All activities enabling the company to have the right human resources, in the right quantity and quality.
Ferrary (2014): A coherent managerial system implementing the human dimension of the organization's strategy.
  • Mechanistic vision: Individual = conditionable resource (like a machine)
  • Individual dimension: Recruitment, Compensation, Evaluation, Career, Training
  • Collective dimension: GPEC, Communication, Working Conditions, Labor Relations, Workforce Reduction

2. HRM as a Shared Function

  • HR Dept: Systems design, policy, administration
  • Managers: Recruit, evaluate, propose training, grant bonuses, support careers
  • Also: General Mgmt, employees, reps, State, unions, consultants

4. Ulrich Model (1997) — 4 HR Roles

  • Strategic Partner: Future + Process → Aligns HR with business
  • Change Agent: Future + People → Drives transformation
  • Administrative Expert: Present + Process → Efficient HR operations
  • Employee Champion: Present + People → Advocates for employees

5. Internal & External Context

  • Internal: Mission, Strategy, Structure, Corporate Culture
  • External: Economic, Technological, Demographic, Socio-cultural changes
Schein's Corporate Culture: 3 levels — Artifacts (visible) → Espoused Values (stated) → Fundamental Assumptions (deep beliefs)

6. Recruitment Process (4 Steps)

Steps: 1) Identify needs → 2) Source candidates → 3) Select candidates → 4) Integrate (onboard)

Job Profile vs. Candidate Profile

  • Job: Title, position, duties, compensation, career prospects
  • Candidate: Education, experience, skills (technical + behavioral), availability

Internal vs. External Recruitment

Internal AdvantagesExternal Advantages
Lower cost, lower risk, easier integrationNew skills, no rivalries, neutral approach
Career development for employeesEnrichment of human resources

Selection Tools

  • Most used: Individual interview (92%) - High acceptability, Weak validity
  • Most valid: Work trial (17%), Aptitude tests (13%) - Strong validity
  • Personality tests: 50% use, Weak validity

Interview Biases

  • Recruiter: Halo effect, Projection bias, Inference effect
  • Candidate: Social desirability bias
Bounded Rationality (Simon): Decisions are made with limited info, time, and cognitive capacity. "Coffee machine system" (Harris & Brewster) = informal influence on hiring.

7. Onboarding

Key stat: 36% of new permanent hires leave within a year!
  • 4 pillars: Inform, Social Integration, Psychological Adaptation, Learning Behaviors

Van Maanen's 7 Socialization Tactics (pairs)

  • Collective vs. Individual
  • Formal vs. Informal
  • Sequential vs. Random
  • Fixed vs. Variable
  • Serial (mentor) vs. Disjunctive (no mentor)
  • Investiture (keep identity) vs. Divestiture (adopt new norms)
  • Competition (merit-based) vs. Tournament (only "best" advance)
