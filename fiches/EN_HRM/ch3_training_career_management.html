Revision Sheet — HRM Ch 3

Training & Career Management

1. Training — Definition & Types

Peretti (2006): Systematic process of equipping employees with knowledge and skills for current jobs and future responsibilities, enhancing organizational performance.
  • Continuous professional training
  • In-company training
  • Digital training (MOOCs)
  • Outsourced training

2. CPF (Personal Training Account)

Key numbers: Created 2014 | 500 euros/year | 15 euros/hour | Max 5,000 euros | Credits kept for life

Professional Transition CPF

  • Replaced CIF in 2019; allows career change via certified training
  • Covers costs + 90% of pay in first year
  • CDI: 24 months experience, 12 in same company
  • CDD: 24 months over 5 years, 4 months CDD in last 12 months

Validation of Prior Experience (VAE)

  • Obtain a diploma/title by validating 1+ year professional experience via jury

3. In-Company Training

Mandatory contributions:
• 0.55% payroll (up to 10 employees)
• 1% payroll (11+ employees)
Paid to OPCOs (replaced OPCAs in 2019)

Skills Development Plan (5 steps)

StepActors
1. PositioningExec Mgmt, HRM
2. Needs analysisExec Mgmt, HRM, Managers, Training Dept, Employees
3. DevelopmentExec Mgmt, HRM, Training Dept, CSE
4. ImplementationHRM, Managers, Training Dept, Employees
5. EvaluationHRM, Training Dept, Employees

Meignant's 4 Training Conceptions

  • Tax: Comply with legal minimums only
  • "Goldmine": Reward/perk without strategic focus
  • Safety: Risk prevention & compliance
  • Development: Strategic, aligned with company goals

4. Career Management

Roger (1991): A continuous process of learning and development through which individuals and organizations jointly plan and manage career paths.

3 Types of Mobility

  • Vertical: Hierarchical change (promotion/demotion)
  • Horizontal: Lateral move (different function, same level)
  • Geographical: Change of location (local/national/international)

5. Career Management Actors

ActorRole
HR DeptProposes tools, defines policy
Exec MgmtValidates orientations
HR SpecialistsLead/drive policy
ManagersEvaluate & guide
EmployeeApply skills, negotiate career plan

6. Career Management Tools

  • Company needs: GPEC, job maps, competency frameworks, career pathways
  • Alignment: Career committees, interviews, development contracts, HP management
  • Individual: Assessments, career counseling
Career Development Interview: Mandatory since 2014, every 2 years. Covers career plan, training, employability, professional future. Conducted by HR (not direct manager).

7. Ference Matrix (1977)

Low PerformanceHigh Performance
High PotentialRising Stars (need development)Stars (top performers)
Low PotentialDead Wood (underperformers)Pillars (reliable, limited growth)

High Potentials Management

  • Rapid promotions
  • Retention incentives
  • Performance testing
  • Commitment assessment

Flash Questions

Q: How much does the CPF credit per year?
A: 500 euros/year, up to a ceiling of 5,000 euros, kept for life
Q: What mandatory training contribution do companies with 11+ employees pay?
A: 1% of payroll, paid to OPCOs
Q: What are Meignant's 4 conceptions of training?
A: Tax, "Goldmine", Safety, Development
Q: What are the 4 quadrants of the Ference Matrix?
A: Stars (high/high), Rising Stars (high potential/low perf), Pillars (low potential/high perf), Dead Wood (low/low)
Q: Name the 3 types of mobility.
A: Vertical (hierarchical), Horizontal (lateral), Geographical (location change)
