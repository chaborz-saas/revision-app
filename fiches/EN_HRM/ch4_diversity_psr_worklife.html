Diversity, Psychosocial Risks & Work-Life Balance

1. Diversity Management

Diversity Management: A strategic approach valuing differences among individuals and creating an inclusive environment enabling all employees to contribute to organizational goals.
Stereotype: Tendency to apply positive/negative characteristics to a person based on general categorization.

3 Pillars of Effective Diversity Management

  • Attract & retain diverse profiles: Highlight diversity in ads, eliminate bias, create supportive climate
  • Diversity promotion programs: Inform managers about legal provisions, show diversity enhances performance
  • Diversity within teams: Overcome own stereotypes, strengthen collective management, individualized approach

3. Gender Inequalities — Key Stats

France: Men earn 22.2% more | Women = 47% workforce but 34% managers, 14% executives | Only 4 women CEOs in CAC 40

Rixain Law (2021)

  • 30% women in leadership by 2026 → 40% by 2030
  • Annual gender data publication required
  • Penalty: up to 1% of wage bill

5. Psychosocial Risks (PSR)

PSR: Risks to mental health from work organization: work pressure, lack of autonomy, poor relationships, conflicting demands → stress, burnout, psychological disorders.

4 Types of Manifestations

  • Stress: Imbalance between perceived demands and perceived resources (ANI, 2008)
  • Burnout: Physical, emotional, mental exhaustion from prolonged demanding situations
  • Bore-out: Chronic suffering from lack of stimulation at work
  • Moral harassment: Psychological harassment in the workplace

Alarming Figures

PSR = 2.6% of GDP | 48% of sick leaves | 400 suicides/year | 24% hyper-stressed | 36% experienced burnout | 20% constantly bored

6. Quality of Work Life

  • Only 12% satisfied with work-life balance; 65% dissatisfied
  • 72% "lack time"; 92% consider it very important
  • 60% say employer "does not do much"

Manager's Role

  • Organization: Fair task distribution, realistic goals, reasonable hours, optimized meetings
  • Support: Recognition, protection, comfort, positive feelings
  • Information: Communicate about work-life balance services
Right to Disconnect (2016): Employees not expected to respond to work communications outside working hours. Prevents cognitive and emotional overload from digital technologies.
