Performance Management • Key concepts & flash questions

Business Purpose & Organisation

  • Primary purpose: Create and keep a customer
  • Primary financial goal: Enhance owner wealth long-term
  • Balance between risk and return

Board of directors (3 roles): (1) Set direction, (2) Monitor & control, (3) Communicate with stakeholders.

Strategic Management — 5 Steps

  1. Mission, vision & objectives
  2. Position analysis (SWOT)
  3. Identify & assess strategic options
  4. Select options & formulate plans (budgets)
  5. Perform, review & control (variance analysis)
SWOT: Strengths & Weaknesses = Internal | Opportunities & Threats = External

Management Accounting — Role

  • Internal, unregulated, future-focused, customisable
  • 4 service roles: Information provider, Advisor, Controller, Partner
4 Principles (IRAT): Influence • Relevance • Analysis • Trust

Ethics

4 Standards: Integrity (truthful) • Objectivity (no bias) • Confidentiality (protect data) • Competence (maintain skill)

Changing Landscape

  • Service sector growth, e-commerce, automation, lean manufacturing, outsourcing
  • Management accountant now: more analysis, business advice, leadership, non-financial information

Flash Questions

Q: What is the primary purpose of a business?
A: To create and keep a customer.
Q: Name the 5 steps of strategic management.
A: Mission/vision/objectives → Position analysis → Identify options → Select & formulate plans → Perform, review & control.
Q: What are the 4 principles of management accounting?
A: Influence, Relevance, Analysis, Trust (IRAT).
Q: How does management accounting differ from financial accounting in time focus?
A: Management accounting is future-focused; financial accounting is historical.
Q: Name the 4 ethical standards for management accountants.
A: Integrity, Objectivity, Confidentiality, Competence.
