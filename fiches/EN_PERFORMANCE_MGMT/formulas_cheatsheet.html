Performance Management — M1 · Formula Cheat Sheet

📐 All Formulas at a Glance

Every key formula from the course — costs, variances, payroll, target costing. Memorise these.

1 · Cost Structure & Break-Even
Contribution Margin (CM)
CM = Revenue − Variable Costs
Also called "Marge sur Coûts Variables" (MCV)
CM Rate (TMCV)
TMCV = CM / Revenue
= (Rev − VC) / Rev
Ex: CM = 400k, Rev = 1M → TMCV = 40%
Break-Even Point (€)
BEP (€) = Fixed Costs / TMCV
Ex: FC = 200k, TMCV = 40% → BEP = 500k€
Break-Even Point (units)
BEP (units) = Fixed Costs / CM per unit
Ex: FC = 200k, CM/u = 0.40€ → BEP = 500k units
Break-Even Date (days)
BEP (days) = (BEP / Annual Rev) × 360
When in the year does profit start?
Safety Margin & Index
SM = Revenue − BEP
SI = (Revenue − BEP) / Revenue
How far above break-even are we?
Net Result (Income Statement)
Result = CM − Fixed Costs
= Revenue × TMCV − FC
VAT (TVA) to Pay
VAT payable = VAT collected − VAT deductible
If deductible > collected → VAT credit (carried forward)
2 · Target Costing
Target Cost
Target Cost = Market Price − Target Margin
= Price × (1 − Margin rate)
Price is set by the market, margin is strategic → cost is the variable
Price excl. tax from TTC
Price HT = Price TTC / (1 + VAT rate)
Ex: 100 000€ TTC, VAT 20% → HT = 83 333€
Gap to Close
Gap = Estimated Cost − Target Cost
Positive = cost too high → must reduce. Negative = cost already below target.
Component Weight (Value Analysis)
Weight(i) = Σ [ Function%(j) × Contribution%(i,j) ]
Sum of: each function's weight × component's contribution to that function
Component Target Cost
Target Cost(i) = Weight(i) × Total Target Cost
Then compare with estimated cost per component
Value (AFNOR)
Value = Needs Satisfied / Costs
Increase value by: cutting useless functions, reducing cost of a function without changing service
3 · Variance Analysis (Écarts)
⚠ Sign convention: Favourable variance = positive contribution to profit (real revenue > forecast OR real cost < forecast). Unfavourable = opposite.
Favourable (F) Unfavourable (U)
3a · Revenue Variances
Total Revenue Variance
= (Qr × Pr) − (Qp × Pp)
Qr = actual qty, Pr = actual price, Qp = planned qty, Pp = planned price
Price Variance (Revenue)
= (Pr − Pp) × Qr
Volume Variance (Revenue)
= (Qr − Qp) × Pp
Activity Variance
= (Total Qr − Total Qp) × Avg Std Margin
Avg Std Margin = Σ [Std margin per unit(i) × Planned mix%(i)]
Mix Variance
= Σ [ (Qr(i) − Qmix(i)) × Std Margin(i) ]
Qmix(i) = planned share(i) × total actual qty
Production Cost Variance
= (Std unit cost − Actual unit cost) × Qr
3b · Direct Cost Variances
Materials — Total Variance (EM)
EM = (Qr × Pr) − (Qp × Pp)
Materials — Usage Variance
= (Qr − Qp) × Pp
More/less material used than standard
Materials — Price Variance
= (Pr − Pp) × Qr
Paid more/less per unit of material than standard
Labour — Total Variance (EMO)
EMO = (Tr × Sr) − (Tp × Sp)
T = time (hours), S = hourly rate
Labour — Rate Variance
= (Sr − Sp) × Tr
Labour — Efficiency Variance
= (Tr − Tp) × Sp
3c · Full Variance Tree
Result Variance Decomposition
Total Result Variance
├── Revenue Variances
│   ├── Price Variance          = (Pr − Pp) × Qr
│   ├── Activity Variance       = (Total Qr − Qp) × Avg Std Margin
│   └── Mix Variance            = Σ[(Qr(i) − Qmix(i)) × Std Margin(i)]
└── Cost Variances
    ├── Direct Materials
    │   ├── Usage Variance      = (Qr − Qp) × Pp
    │   └── Price Variance      = (Pr − Pp) × Qr
    └── Direct Labour
        ├── Efficiency Variance = (Tr − Tp) × Sp
        └── Rate Variance       = (Sr − Sp) × Tr
4 · Standard Costing
Standard Cost
Std Cost = Std Qty × Std Unit Cost
Budget Control
Variance = Actual Result − Budgeted Result
Compare YTD actuals vs cumulative budget. Also track Year-To-Go (reste à faire).
5 · Payroll & HR Controlling
Payroll Mass Variation — Additive
Δ Payroll = Δ Headcount effect + Δ Pay level
Payroll Mass Variation — Multiplicative
(1 + Δ Headcount) × (1 + Δ Pay) = (1 + Δ Payroll)
Ex: +10% headcount, +5% pay → 1.10 × 1.05 = 1.155 → +15.5%
Pay Increase — In-Year Effect (Masse)
Mass effect = % increase × (months remaining / 12)
Ex: +4% on Oct 1 → mass = 4% × 3/12 = 1%
Pay Increase — Carry-Over to Next Year
Carry-over = (Full-year cost N+1 − In-year mass N) / Payroll N
Noria Effect (turnover savings)
Noria = (Avg salary joiners − Avg salary leavers) × nb replacements × prorata
Ex: 10 replacements, joiners 20k, leavers 40k → −200k€ saving
Absenteeism Rate
Abs. rate = Days absent / Days paid × 100
GVT (Individual Pay Drift)
G = Merit (individual performance) V = Seniority increments T = Skills / job upgrades
GVT = automatic upward payroll drift even without collective raises

KPI Formula What it measures
Profitability Profit / Capital invested Return on investment
Productivity Output / Input Resource efficiency

Safety Index (Rev − BEP) / Rev Buffer above break-even
Accident frequency rate Accidents × 1 000 000 / Hours worked Workplace safety
Accident severity rate Days lost × 1 000 / Hours worked Severity of accidents
Payroll / Revenue Total payroll cost / Revenue Labour cost intensity
Training spend % Training budget / Gross payroll HR investment in skills

7 · Quick Reference — All Variances
Variance Formula Favourable if...
Revenue — Price (Pr − Pp) × Qr Actual price > Planned price
Revenue — Volume (Qr − Qp) × Pp Actual qty > Planned qty
Revenue — Activity (Total Qr − Qp) × Avg Std Margin Sold more than planned overall
Revenue — Mix Σ[(Qr(i) − Qmix(i)) × Std Margin(i)] Shifted toward high-margin products
Materials — Usage (Qr − Qp) × Pp Used less material than standard
Materials — Price (Pr − Pp) × Qr Paid less per unit than standard
Labour — Efficiency (Tr − Tp) × Sp Worked fewer hours than standard
Labour — Rate (Sr − Sp) × Tr Hourly rate lower than standard
Target Cost Gap Estimated Cost − Target Cost Negative (cost below target)
