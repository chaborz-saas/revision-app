Relationship Marketing — Chapter 1

Fundamentals, Satisfaction & Customer Experience

1. Key Definitions
Relationship Marketing = All marketing activities aimed at establishing, developing and maintaining lasting relationships with the best customers (Morgan & Hunt, 1994)
Transactional Marketing = Focus on the current sale, short-term, acquisition only. Opposite of relational approach.
CRM = Same as Relationship Marketing, but with a more strategic/financial and IS connotation. Creates "customer equity".
Satisfaction = Feeling from comparing prior expectations vs. actual experience (Oliver's model)
Customer Experience (CX) = Sum of all functional, sensory, emotional experiences with a brand
Customer Journey = Process through all stages and contact points making up the CX (Lemon & Verhoef, 2016)

3. Two Strategic Objectives
Acquisition: Expensive but necessary. Select the "right" prospects.
Loyalty: Increase profitability, extend relationship duration. Costs less than acquisition.
Both compete for the same marketing budget. Finding the right balance is critical.
4. Three CRM Targets
  • Current customers (retain & develop)
  • Former customers (win back)
  • Prospects (acquire selectively)
5. Satisfaction & Loyalty
Oliver's model: Satisfaction = Experience > Expectations. Dissatisfaction = Experience < Expectations.
Satisfaction does NOT guarantee loyalty. In USA: 90% satisfied with car brand, only 35% repurchase.
5 benefits of loyal customers: (1) Lower retention cost (2) Buy more (3) Buy regularly (4) Positive WOM (5) Reduce support costs
Loyalty TypeMeaning
AbsoluteContinues to buy the brand
RelativeBuys but not exclusively (Share of Wallet < 100%)
ObjectiveObserved behavior; may be passive or forced
SubjectiveTrue positive mental attitudes / emotional attachment
A satisfied customer may leave due to: competitor solicitation, desire for variety, low switching costs, or satisfaction not equaling preference.
6. Customer Journey & Omnichannel
Full journey: Awareness > Consideration > Purchase > Use > Support > Advocacy > Elimination. Do NOT underestimate the USE phase.
EvolutionDescription
MultichannelIndependent contact points
Cross-channelStreamlined journey (Click & Collect)
OmnichannelPoints in synergy, maximum result
Customer typology (Herhausen 2019): Physical addicts, Online pragmatics, Online intensives, Multi-channel enthusiasts, O2O. Multi-channel types need seamless journey for loyalty.
7. Experiential Models
Schmitt's 5 SEMs: SENSE (sensory) + FEEL (affective) + THINK (cognitive) + ACT (behavioral) + RELATE (social)
Hetzel's Experiential Wheel: Surprise + Offer the extraordinary + Stimulate 5 senses + Create a link + Immerse in brand universe
8. Flash Questions
What is the definition of Relationship Marketing?
All marketing activities aimed at establishing, developing and maintaining lasting relationships with the best customers (Morgan & Hunt, 1994)
What are the 5 benefits of loyal customers?
1) Lower cost than acquisition 2) Buy more 3) Buy regularly 4) Positive word-of-mouth 5) Reduce support costs
Why doesn't satisfaction always lead to loyalty?
Competitor solicitation, desire for variety, low switching costs, satisfaction =/= preference
What are Schmitt's 5 SEMs?
SENSE (sensory), FEEL (affective), THINK (cognitive), ACT (behavioral), RELATE (social/relational)
Multichannel vs. Cross-channel vs. Omnichannel?
Multi = independent channels. Cross = streamlined (Click&Collect). Omni = synergy to maximize results.
