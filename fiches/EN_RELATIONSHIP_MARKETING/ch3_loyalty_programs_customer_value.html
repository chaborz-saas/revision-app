Relationship Marketing — Chapter 3

Loyalty Programs & Customer Value Strategy

1. Loyalty Programs: Types
TypeMechanism
Content marketingNewsletters, social media. Must be useful/interesting.
Points-based rewardsEarn points, convert into rewards
Status-based rewardsTiers: Silver/Gold/Platinum. Encourages increase in value.
Paid membershipRegistration fee filters committed customers (Amazon Prime)
PartnershipsCross-brand rewards, cross-selling opportunities
2. The 3 Levers
Accelerate (Rewards) = Move customers from lower to higher spending levels
Support (Relationship) = Personalized assistance, strong brand influence
Retain (Status) = Discourage backward movement, significant benefits for top customers
3. Key Loyalty KPIs
Attrition Rate = Customers lost / Customers at start. Example: (10K-9K)/10K = 10%
Retention Rate = 100% - Attrition Rate. Example: 90%
NPS = % Promoters (9-10) - % Detractors (0-6). Good: 50%+. Excellent: ~75%.
Customer Lifetime = 1 / Attrition Rate. Example: 1/10% = 10 years
4. Limits of Loyalty Programs
Cost underestimated. Competitor imitation destroys advantage. Unclear if loyalty is to the program or the brand.
5. CRM: From Revenue to Profit
Traditional: Customer = income source. CRM: Profitable customer = profit source. (Cova & Cova)
RFM Analysis: Recency x Frequency x Monetary amount. Key customer data to capture.
8 CRM activities: 1) Capture info 2) Centralize in repository 3) Analyze value 4) Share across channels 5) Individualized solutions 6) Robotize offers 7) Individualized service 8) Control costs
6. Three Required CRM Cultures
CulturePrinciple
CustomerCustomer satisfaction inspires everyone
StrategicOptimize CLV, not immediate sale profit
EconomicSome customers are more profitable. Increase active x avg value = firm value
COS (Customer Orientation Score): Measures perception gap between employees (66%) and customers (42%). Benchmark: 55%.
7. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)
CLV = Customer Value/year x Customer Lifetime = (Revenue - Costs) x (1/Attrition Rate)
Principle: Total purchases - Acquisition & retention expenses = Customer NPV
"Elevator model": Lose money on acquisition (Year 1), profit on recurring maintenance. Hard financed by Soft.
8. Targeting Warnings
Poorly targeted acquisition promotions also hit existing customers, causing: (1) cannibalization (substitute promo for old contract) and (2) "Wake-up effect" (customers notice competitors).
Loyalty managed by mastering BOTH Targeting AND Effectiveness. Example: 20% targeted x 50% effectiveness = 10% saved, lifetime goes from 3.3 to 5 years.
9. Flash Questions
What are the 3 levers of loyalty programs?
Accelerate (Rewards), Support (Relationship), Retain (Status)
How do you calculate CLV?
CLV = (Customer Revenue - Acquisition & Loyalty Costs) x (1 / Attrition Rate)
What is NPS and what is a good score?
NPS = % Promoters - % Detractors. Good: 50%+. Excellent: ~75%.
What are the 3 cultures required for CRM?
Customer culture, Strategic culture (optimize CLV not single sale), Economic culture (not all customers equal)
What is the "Wake-up effect"?
When acquisition promotions reach existing customers, they "wake up" and start looking at competitors, potentially leaving.
