2. Intelligent Agents
Definition: Autonomous systems that perceive environment, make decisions, act, and learn from experience
  • Monoprix: generative chatbot on FAQ + CRM data
  • Real-time newsletter personalization
  • Recommendation engines with push notifications
3. The 3 Levels of AI (Huang et al., 2023)
LevelNameFunctionExamples
AMechanical AIExecutes tasksChatbots, dynamic pricing
BThinking AIAnalyzes dataRecommender systems, predictive churn
CFeeling AIRecognizes emotionsSentiment analysis, facial emotion detection
AI first augments then replaces human intelligence at each level. Sephora ColorIQ = Thinking AI. Nike Fit = Mechanical + Thinking.
4. In-Store Tech for RM
Smart shelves: Real-time promotions & inventory. Mobile checkout: Reduced friction. AR: Product visualization. AI scanning: Nike Fit shoe sizing.
Grewal et al. framework (2023): Two dimensions: Efficiency (faster, cheaper, smoother) + Enhancement (richer, more engaging experiences). Tech must serve both.
5. AI and "Real" Relationships
AI can simulate empathy but does not truly feel. Trust is the critical success factor. The authentic vs. simulated line is blurring.
BenefitsDrawbacks
For businessesCost reduction, scalability, 24/7, personalization at scaleImplementation cost, loss of human touch, data privacy
For customersFaster service, personalized, consistentPrivacy concerns, lack of empathy, frustration when AI fails
6. Collaborative Intelligence
Humans: Empathy, context, intuition, moral values. AI: Speed, volume, pattern recognition, 24/7.
Key principle: Be an ARCHITECT of combined AI + human use, not just a basic user.
Schumpeter's Creative Destruction: AI is revolutionizing CRM from within, destroying old practices and creating new ones.
7. Key Conclusions
1) AI transforms RM but doesn't totally replace humans. 2) Trust is key. 3) Tech must align with customer AND employee journey. 4) Be architects of hybrid intelligence.
8. Flash Questions
What are the 3 levels of AI according to Huang et al. (2023)?
A) Mechanical (executes tasks) B) Thinking (analyzes data) C) Feeling (recognizes emotions). AI first augments then replaces human intelligence at each level.
What is Collaborative Intelligence?
Combining human strengths (empathy, intuition, moral values) with AI strengths (speed, volume, pattern recognition). Be an architect of hybrid use.
What are the two dimensions of the Grewal et al. in-store tech framework?
Efficiency (faster, cheaper, smoother processes) and Enhancement (richer, more engaging experiences). Technology should serve both.
What is the key success factor for AI in Relationship Marketing?
Trust. Customers must trust AI systems for them to be effective in building and maintaining relationships.
