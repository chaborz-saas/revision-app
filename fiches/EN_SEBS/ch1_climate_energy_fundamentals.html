SUSTAINABLE & ETHICAL BUSINESS STRATEGY

Revision Sheet — Ch 1: Climate & Energy Fundamentals

1. Sustainable Development

Definition
  • Meeting the needs of current generations without compromising future generations (EU, 2008)
  • Two central notions: economic development (not just growth) + sustainability (intergenerational view)
  • 3 pillars: Social, Economy, Environment
Flash Q: What are the 3 pillars of SD and what sits at their intersection?
Social + Economy = Equitable | Economy + Environment = Viable | Environment + Social = Liveable | All 3 = Sustainable

2. The Anthropocene

  • Proposed geological epoch where humans dominate Earth's systems
  • Coined by Paul Crutzen (Nobel Prize), early 2000s
  • Markers: climate change, biodiversity loss, pollution, land use changes, geological traces
  • The Great Acceleration: massive extraction + energy use + biosphere transformation

3. Systems Thinking

  • The world = complex system of interconnected sub-systems
  • Lake/Fish/Fishermen parable: 3 outcomes — stabilization, oscillation, or collapse
  • Human society = a "processing machine" (resources in, waste out)
  • Traditional economic indicators assume infinite resources — flawed assumption
  • Circular economy: only 9.3 Gt recycled vs. 67.4 Gt waste globally

4. Planetary Boundaries

6 of 9 boundaries exceeded: Climate, biodiversity, biogeochemical flows, land use, freshwater, novel entities. Remaining safe: ocean acidification, ozone. Uncertain: aerosols.

Flash Q: What is the "carbon double constraint"?
Fossil fuels face: (1) Scarcity of reserves + (2) GHG emissions causing climate change.

6. Energy Essentials

Power vs Energy
  • Power (kW) = rate of energy use (like speed)
  • Energy (kWh) = total work done (like distance)
  • Energy = Power x Time
Energy Mix Facts
  • Fossil fuels still dominate the global mix
  • Population x6, energy use x30
  • Renewables face intermittency (the main problem) and storage challenges
  • Solar radiation = 10,000x human consumption but hard to capture/store

7. Climate Change Key Points

  • Climate is not weather (different timescales)
  • Greenhouse effect is natural but amplified by humans
  • Small temperature changes = big consequences
  • Speed of change is unprecedented
IPCC (AR5): (1) Humans cause accelerated climate change. (2) Limit warming to +2 degrees C. (3) Beyond that = risk of runaway climate change.

8. Priority Order for Action

  1. Sobriety (reduce needs)
  2. Energy efficiency (do more with less)
  3. Renewable energy (clean sources)

The society of "always more" must shift to "always better."

Flash Q: Name the 9 planetary boundaries.
Climate change, biosphere integrity, biogeochemical flows, land-system change, freshwater use, ocean acidification, ozone depletion, aerosol loading, novel entities.
