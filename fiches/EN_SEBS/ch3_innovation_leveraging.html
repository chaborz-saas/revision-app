SUSTAINABLE & ETHICAL BUSINESS STRATEGY

Revision Sheet — Ch 3: Innovation & Leveraging

1. What Is Innovation?

4 Traditional Categories
CategoryDescriptionExample
ProductNew or improved goods/services-
ProcessImproving how things are made/deliveredToyota Lean Production
OrganizationalChanging structures/managementGoogle flat management
MarketNew markets or positioningNetflix streaming

These are interconnected, but business model innovation is the real competitive advantage.

Core message: Competitiveness = business model innovation. Real innovation that works requires boldness to change the business model, not just improve products.
Flash Q: What differentiates BlaBlaCar's innovation?
Not the technology (website + geolocation existed), but the business model of carpooling between individuals — creating a new collaborative mobility market.

2. Business Model Innovation Examples

BlaBlaCar
  • Technology was not new (website + geolocation)
  • Innovation = business model of peer-to-peer carpooling
  • Created a new collaborative mobility market
Decathlon
  • Sports equipment already existed
  • Innovation = vertical integration (design + produce + sell own brands under one roof)
  • Reduced prices, democratized sport access, global leader

3. Prosumption & Digital Transformation

  • Prosumption: People are both producers AND consumers simultaneously
  • Enabled by: social media, 3D printing, YouTube, e-commerce platforms
  • Means of creation, production, distribution now accessible to all
  • Vinted: #1 clothing seller in France by volume; second-hand = 10.9% of clothing by value, 16.3% among 18-34 year-olds

4. Toffler's Three Waves

WaveAgeKey Resource
1stAgriculturalLand, manual labor
2ndIndustrialMachines, factories
3rdDigital/InformationInformation, social capital
3rd Wave Characteristics
  • Key organisation: Platforms (Uber, Airbnb, YouTube)
  • Roles: Prosumers (both produce and consume)
  • Production: Customized instead of mass-produced
  • Economy: Decentralized, digitally connected
  • Society: Network-based, diverse
Flash Q: What is social capital and why is it key?
Social capital = connections, data, trust. In platform economies, it's the key resource. New business models must engage all stakeholders — especially for societal transformation.

5. Circular Economy in Practice

  • SOJO: App connecting consumers with local tailors for repairs/alterations — extends garment lifespan
  • Wrote Farm: Sustainable agriculture regenerating local ecosystems while creating economic value
  • Both show how innovation = new business strategy, not just new technology
Summary: Real innovation = business model change. Digital enables prosumption & platforms. Social capital is the new competitive foundation. The shift is from mass production to personalized, collaborative, sustainable value creation.
