Flash Q: What does ASI stand for in sustainable transport?
Avoid (reduce need), Shift (to cleaner modes), Improve (efficiency of remaining transport).

2. Rethink Energy (Part 13)

Two Pillars
  1. De-carbonated electricity (renewables, nuclear)
  2. New molecules (especially green hydrogen)
5 Levers for Transformation
  • Reduce energy needs (sobriety)
  • Electrify (transport, heating)
  • De-carbonate production
  • Storage / new molecules (hydrogen, batteries)
  • New industrial processes
Key Technologies
  • Agrivoltaism: Solar + agriculture on same land. Nuclear equivalent = just 2% of French farmland
  • Gen IV nuclear: Breeder reactors + thorium
  • ATES: Aquifer Thermal Energy Storage for seasonal demand
  • Green hydrogen: Electrolysis from renewables; connects energy, transport, industry
Flash Q: What makes hydrogen a "full rethink" of energy?
Multiple supply pathways, storage/transport options, and end uses — it connects renewables, electricity grids, gas networks, industry, and transport into one system.

3. Reduce Resource Consumption (Part 14)

Ownership to Service
  • Economy of service functionality: Sell the service, not the product
  • Incentivizes durability, repairability, efficiency
  • Example: Energy Performance Contracts (since 1937)
Sustainable Design
  • Adjustable, Repairable, Sustainable (durable)
  • Bio-based materials: cactus, grape, mushroom, pineapple, cork, hemp, etc.
  • Upcycling: waste → higher-value products
  • Bioplastics replacing petroleum-based plastics

4. Regenerate Biosphere (Part 15)

  • Bioremediation: Using living organisms (bacteria, fungi, plants) to clean contaminated environments

5. Feed Humanity (Part 16)

  • Agriculture 4.0: Sensors, drones, AI, precision farming
  • Proteins from thin air: Microorganisms + CO2 + water + electricity
  • Valorize waste streams and underutilized bio-resources

6. Educate (Part 17)

  • Continuous learning for workers, managers, citizens
  • Knowledge exchange across sectors and borders
"New Abundance" (BCG): Resource constraints = strategic opportunities. Companies ignoring physical constraints face existential risks. Those adapting gain competitive advantages. A sustainable strategy must account for physical constraints, not just financial ones.
Flash Q: Name 3 benefits of biochar.
(1) Locks carbon in soil for centuries, (2) Produces clean energy during creation, (3) Improves soil fertility and water retention.
