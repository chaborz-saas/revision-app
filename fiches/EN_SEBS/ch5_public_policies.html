Flash Q: Name the IEA's 3 scenarios.
(1) Current Policies (business-as-usual, >3-4 degrees C), (2) Stated Policies (existing commitments, insufficient), (3) Sustainable Development (Paris-aligned, only one that limits warming).

2. Carbon Neutral vs. Net Zero vs. Absolute Zero

ConceptDefinition
Carbon NeutralOffset emissions via external projects (trees, credits). No deep transformation required.
Net ZeroMajor reductions across Scope 1, 2, 3. Minimal offsetting. Science-based (1.5 degrees C).
Absolute ZeroZero emissions with NO removal/offsetting. Most stringent target.
Greenwashing risk: "Carbon neutral" claims can mask lack of real emission reductions. True ambition = Net Zero (or Absolute Zero).

3. GHG Protocol — Scope 1, 2, 3

  • Scope 1: Direct emissions (owned sources — factories, vehicles)
  • Scope 2: Indirect energy emissions (purchased electricity, heating)
  • Scope 3: Other indirect (supply chain, commuting, product use, waste) — usually the largest share
Flash Q: What percentage of emissions may be "missing" from national accounts?
~25%. Many countries inflate carbon absorption claims (e.g., Malaysia subtracted 73% of its emissions via questionable forestry claims).

5. EU Taxonomy

4 Principles
  • Substantial Contribution: Must advance 1 of 6 environmental objectives
  • DNSH: Do No Significant Harm to other objectives
  • Minimum Social Safeguards: Workers' rights & ethics
  • Technical Screening Criteria: EU-defined compliance standards

6 Objectives: Climate mitigation, climate adaptation, water/marine protection, circular economy, pollution prevention, biodiversity/ecosystem restoration.

6. CSRD & Double Materiality

Double Materiality
  • Financial materiality (Outside → Inside): How sustainability issues affect the company financially
  • Impact materiality (Inside → Outside): How the company affects people & environment
  • = "impact ON the company" + "impact OF the company"

7. CSRD Reporting Topics

  • Environment: Climate, pollution, water, biodiversity, circular economy
  • Social: Workforce, value chain workers, communities, consumers
  • Governance: Business conduct

Implemented via ESRS (European Sustainability Reporting Standards). Sector-specific ESRS still in development.

Flash Q: What is double materiality?
Reporting from TWO perspectives: (1) how sustainability issues affect the company (financial), and (2) how the company affects people & environment (impact). Core innovation of CSRD.
