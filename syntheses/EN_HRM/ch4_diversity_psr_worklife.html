Summary — HRM Ch 4: Diversity, Psychosocial Risks & Work-Life Balance

1. Diversity Management

Diversity: All kinds of visible and non-visible differences between individuals.
Diversity Management (Pearson Education, 2015): A strategic approach to managing a diverse workforce by valuing differences among individuals and creating an inclusive environment that enables all employees to contribute to organizational goals.
Stereotype: The tendency to apply positive or negative characteristics to a person based on a general categorization and perceived similarities.

1.2 How to Manage Diversity Effectively

Three interconnected approaches:

1.2.1 Attracting, Recruiting, Developing & Retaining Diverse Profiles

  • Highlight diversity in recruitment advertisements
  • Ensure the absence of recruitment bias
  • Create a climate that supports diversity

1.2.2 Diversity Promotion Programs

  • Inform managers about legal provisions supporting equal opportunities and encourage fair treatment
  • Show managers that diversity enhances performance

1.2.3 Diversity Within Teams — Manager Implications

  • Reflect on one's own stereotypes and overcome them to gain objectivity
  • Strengthen the collective aspect of management
  • Practice an individualized approach through understanding and respecting each person's specificities

2. Focus on Gender Inequalities

Key Statistics on Gender Inequality in France:
  • Average salary of men is 22.2% higher than that of women (INSEE, 2023)
  • Women represent 47% of the active workforce but only 34% of managerial positions
  • Women hold only 14% of executive roles
  • Only 4 women CEOs of a CAC 40 company
  • Women are concentrated in female-dominated professions: health, social work, education, personal care, communication

2.1 Rixain Law (2021)

  • Gender Quotas in Leadership: At least 30% of executive/senior management positions held by women by 2026, increasing to 40% by 2030
  • Transparency & Reporting: Companies must annually publish data on gender balance in leadership teams
  • Penalties: Up to 1% of total wage bill for non-compliance
  • Objective: Break the "glass ceiling" and promote equal opportunities in decision-making roles (only 26% of leadership positions in 2024)

2.2 Institutional Isomorphism (DiMaggio & Powell, 1983)

Recruitment Insight: Research by Johnson, Hekman & Chan (2016) published in Harvard Business Review found that if there is only one woman in a candidate pool, there is statistically no chance she will be hired. This highlights the importance of building diverse candidate shortlists.

3. Psychosocial Risks (PSR)

Psychosocial Risks (Besseyre des Horts, 2011): Risks to employees' mental health and well-being that arise from the organization and management of work, including factors such as work pressure, lack of autonomy, poor social relationships, and conflicting demands, which can lead to stress, burnout, and other psychological disorders.

3.3 Alarming Figures in France

Key Statistics:
  • PSR cost amounts to 2.6% of GDP (Mozart Consulting)
  • PSRs account for 48% of sick leaves in large companies (AT-MP Barometer, 2018)
  • 400 work-related suicides per year (Assurance Maladie, 2018)
  • 20% of employees report feeling bored at work constantly (QAPA)
  • 24% of employees consider themselves in a state of hyper-stress (Stimulus, 2017)
  • 36% of employees report having experienced burnout (CFDT, 2017)

4. Quality of Work Life & Work-Life Balance

4.1 Current Situation

  • Only 12% of employees are satisfied with work-life balance; 65% are dissatisfied (Robert Half, 2018)
  • 72% feel they "lack time" daily
  • 92% consider work-life balance an important or very important issue
  • For 60% of employees, their employer "does not do much" to help them balance work and personal life (OPE Barometer, 2018)

4.2 Benefits for Companies

  • Improvement of social climate and quality of work life
  • Increased motivation and satisfaction among employees
  • Greater focus and better productivity
  • Reduction in absenteeism, turnover, and overtime hours
  • Enhanced attractiveness and stronger employee retention
Two essential conditions: Employees must have a sense of responsibility and autonomy, and the employer must trust them.

4.3 Organizational Policies for Work-Life Balance

ThemePolicies
Work flexibilityTemporal flexibility (work hours, compressed workweek, part-time, job sharing); Spatial flexibility (telecommuting/remote work); Career flexibility (leaves and career breaks based on family/professional events)
Resources outside of workInfrastructure (company daycare, concierge services); Information and advice (health/wellness prevention, conferences on healthy living, addiction); Assistance with daily life (legal, logistical, psychological support); Financial aid

4.4 How Managers Can Promote Work-Life Balance

  • Work organization level: Equitable distribution of tasks, realistic goals and deadlines, reasonable working hours, optimized meetings, proper use of emails
  • Employee support level: Providing recognition, protection, comfort, expressing positive feelings
  • Information level: Advice and information about the company's work-life balance services

4.5 The Right to Disconnect

Right to Disconnect (Labor Law, August 8, 2016): Aims to better respect the balance between personal and professional life. The use of digital technologies can lead to risks of cognitive and emotional overload (Guillot-Soulez, 2017). This right ensures employees are not expected to respond to work communications outside of working hours.

4.6 Schein's Corporate Culture Model Applied to D&I

Understanding corporate culture through Schein's three levels helps identify where diversity and inclusion efforts must penetrate:

  1. Artifacts (Symbols): Visible signs of inclusive culture — diverse imagery, inclusive language in communications, accessible spaces
  2. Espoused Values: Stated commitments to diversity, published D&I policies, equality charters
  3. Fundamental Assumptions: Deep beliefs about equality, fairness, and human value that truly drive behavior — the hardest level to change
